📐 Standard Approach (Normal)

  1. First Report (60 or 50 NM): Instruct to report 25 NM. (60→25 = +7m, 50→25 = +5m)
  2. 25 NM: Instruct to report 10 NM. (25→10 = +3m)
  3. 10 NM: Clear for ILS. Flies +2m to AYT VOR.
  4. AYT VOR: Flies +2m outbound to 10 DME FIX.
  5. 10 DME FIX: Flies +5m procedure/base turn → Full Established & Sent to TWR (118.1).

⚡ Straight-In Exceptions (Airway VA-16)

  • RWY 36 Active + South (DERYA / R-170): Straight-In to RWY 36
  • RWY 18 Active + North (KUMRU / R-350): Straight-In to RWY 18
  1. First Report (60 or 50 NM): Instruct to report 25 NM. (60→25 = +7m, 50→25 = +5m)
  2. 25 NM: Clear for ILS directly!
  3. 10 NM (+3m after 25 NM): Full Established & Sent to TWR (118.1) (No VOR overhead or 5-min procedure turn).
